AAHL PRIVACY POLICY

Angry Alpaca Hockey League · theaahl.ca · Last updated October 19, 2026

What we collect

  • Your email address and name, entered at sign-up or provided by Google, Microsoft or Facebook when you choose to sign in with them.
  • Your team name, picks, roster moves and payment status for the pool.

How we use it

  • To match you to your pool team(s) and let you view and manage them.
  • To send pool emails (confirmations, roster move updates, weekly summaries).
  • Team names and points are shown publicly in the standings. Email addresses are never shown publicly.

What we don't do

  • We never sell or share your information with anyone.
  • We don't post to Facebook or access your friends, photos or other account data.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and sign-in) and Cloudflare (website services). Sign-in is optional; you can always use your Entry ID and email instead.

Delete your data

To delete your sign-in account or all of your pool data, email the commissioner at matt.hope001@gmail.com with the subject "AAHL data deletion" and the email you use for the pool. Your data will be removed within 30 days and you'll get a confirmation email.

To remove AAHL's access from Facebook: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → AAHL → Remove.
